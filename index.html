Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Guarantee

SalaryNest is built with an offline-first, client-side architecture. Your sensitive financial numbers, salary slips, and attendance logs are saved securely inside your device sandbox.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SalaryNest, we collect inputs you enter, including your base salary, hourly rates, overtime logs, attendance marks (present, leaves, half-days), allowances, and deductions.

If you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo strictly for authentication and cloud sync purposes.

2. Local Storage & Security

Data is stored in your device's browser/application storage. We do not sell, rent, or share your financial records with advertisers, employers, or third parties.

All salary reports generated remain on your device unless you explicitly share them via WhatsApp, Email, or other apps.

3. Analytics & Advertising

We do not track keystrokes or sensitive wage figures. SalaryNest Pro offers an entirely ad-free experience.

4. Data Ownership & Control

You retain full ownership of your data. You can export a JSON backup of your records or wipe your data completely from the Profile & Settings menu at any time.

5. Contact Information

For any privacy questions or data requests, reach out via the Contact Support option or write to our support team.
